The isolated-note slices come from joetessy/qanun:
https://github.com/joetessy/qanun/tree/781775a018735ba9e7f83f9d84d8ef326bcbc5f2/public/samples/qanun

The keyboard uses the original per-note M4A slices retained by Rawl,
byte-for-byte from the pinned bank. It uses Rawl's per-sample pitch calibration
in playback and transposes the samples for intermediate and out-of-range pitches.
https://rawl.rocks/audio/qanun/SOURCES.md
